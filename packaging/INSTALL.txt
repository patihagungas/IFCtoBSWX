IFC to BSWX Converter - installation
=====================================

1. Extract this whole zip to a folder (right-click > Extract All...).
2. Double-click  Install.cmd
   - installs to  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\IFCtoBSWX  (no administrator rights needed)
   - adds "IFC to BSWX Converter" to the Start menu and the Desktop
   - adds the app to "Open with" for .ifc files
3. Start the app, click "Add files...", pick your IFC files and press Convert.

If Windows SmartScreen warns about the unsigned program, choose "More info" > "Run anyway".

Uninstall: Settings > Apps > Installed apps > "IFC to BSWX Converter" > Uninstall.

Command line (no window):
  "%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\IFCtoBSWX\IFCtoBSWX.exe" --cli model.ifc -o C:\out
  (progress is written to %TEMP%\IFCtoBSWX-cli.log)
